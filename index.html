Vietnamese banknotes you can mix up

  • 20,000 and 500,000 are both blue. This is the most common mistake and the base of the “note swap” trick.
  • 10,000 and 200,000 look similar in dim light (brown-yellow vs orange-red).
  • Say the amount out loud when you pay with a big note, and count your change.

FAQ

What does “50k” mean on a menu?

50,000 dong. Vietnam also writes it as 50.000, with a dot as the thousands separator.

How much is 100,000 dong?

Roughly ₹370, A$5.5, ¥610 or US$3.9. The converter above uses today's rate.

Do I need to tip in Vietnam?

Not at local eateries. At spas and on tours, 20k–50k ₫ is appreciated. Check the bill for a service charge first.
